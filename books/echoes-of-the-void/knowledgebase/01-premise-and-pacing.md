# Premise & Pacing

## Premise

**Elarion Voss** died alone on modern Earth — gay, ignored, emotionally abandoned. Reborn as the unwanted spare omega of minor House Voss, he remembers fragments of his first life. He wants only peace and genuine connection. The Valerian Empire offers him the Academy as an alliance bridge; his family offers him nothing else.

He is painfully average: medium features, basic mage training, one common low-tier Skill he may abandon. No prophecy names him. No void power whispers at birth.

Across secret time loops (resetting each time he dies, always to Academy arrival morning), he experiments with every prince, every alliance, every cruelty and mercy. Some loops contain love, mpreg, children. Their loss breaks him further. Eventually he becomes **the End** — not madness, but exhausted acceptance of absolute loneliness.

## POV rotation

One POV per chapter. Rotate among the 10 mains plus supporting cast. Elarion is frequent but never exclusive. Other characters must carry full chapters where the reader forgets Elarion exists.

## Tournament sequence

1. **Academy years** (17 → 18)
2. **Royal Consort Tournament** — princes date and choose omegas
3. **Crown Prince Couple Tournament** — five settled pairs compete with equal resources
4. Winner becomes Crown Prince + Consort
