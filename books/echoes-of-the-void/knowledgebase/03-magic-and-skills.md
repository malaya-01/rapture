# Magic & Skills

## Absolute rule

A character is **either** a mage **or** a Skill user. **Not both.**

## Mages (Harry Potter model)

External mana manipulation through learned craft:

- Wands, spoken incantations, gesture patterns
- Potion brewing, rune circles, ritual magic
- Familiars, enchanted objects, grimoires
- Dueling magic, magical law, research

**Growth:** Study, practice, invention, rare artifacts.  
**No:** Status screens, Skill slots, body reinforcement Skills.

## Skill users (Overlord model)

Internalized mana as streamlined abilities:

- Body reinforcement, weapon arts, martial techniques
- Passive buffs, active combat Skills, inventory-type Skills
- Racial/class Skills; some unique, many **acquired** through training, dungeons, items

**Growth:** Training, dungeon drops, mentors, leveling through experience.  
**No:** Wand spellcasting, potion mastery as primary path.

## Balance

Master duelist can kill master mage. Master mage can annihilate master warrior. Terrain, preparation, artifacts, and intelligence decide outcomes.

**Hybrids:** Extremely rare; not among main cast unless late plot demands heavy cost.

## Artifacts & dungeons

Ancient relics, dungeon cores, cursed blades, royal regalia. Dungeons: ecosystems, traps, bosses, party dynamics, loot. Artifacts amplify either path; never convert paths.

## Elarion's progression

| Stage | Power |
|-------|-------|
| Loop 1 start | Basic mage + optional common Skill |
| Early loops | Optimization through knowledge |
| Mid loops | Strategic violence; acquired Skills/items |
| Endgame | Termination reality-warp — wrong, apocalyptic, earned |
