# Volume 08 — The Absolute End

**Chapters 316–360** | Act IV: The End

## Volume purpose

War on gods. Termination powers. Bitter-sweet finale — peace through oblivion.

## Key beats

- Elarion stops seeking approval entirely
- Reality frays; End powers named and shown
- Gods as bored cruel audience — destroyed
- Final choice: not revenge, exhausted acceptance
- Universe ends. Quiet. He wanted normal life; he gets empty peace.
