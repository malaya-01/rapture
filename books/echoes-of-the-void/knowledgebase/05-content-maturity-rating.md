# Content Maturity (18+)

Echoes of the Void is **adult fiction**.

## Include honestly

- Graphic violence and gore when plot demands
- Explicit MM romance, heat/rut, mpreg, birth scenes (18+ characters only)
- Psychological horror, existential dread, loop trauma
- Political cruelty, betrayal, child neglect in **backstory** (not on-page minor abuse)

## Do not

- Sexualize minors
- Write torture porn without narrative purpose
- Fade critical emotional beats that define character arcs

## Tone

Bitter-sweet. Beauty and tenderness exist so their loss devastates. Explicit intimacy advances plot and character — especially loop-deja-vu horror during sex.
