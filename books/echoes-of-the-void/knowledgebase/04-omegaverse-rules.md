# Omegaverse & Romance Rules

## Biology

Secondary genders: Alpha, Beta, Omega (male and female variants). Heats, ruts, knotting, slick, scenting, bonding, **mpreg** (male omega pregnancy) are real.

## Romantic attraction — ONLY these pairs

- Male Alpha × Male/Female Omega
- Male Alpha × Female Beta / Female Alpha
- Male Beta × Male/Female Omega / Female Alpha / Female Beta
- Male Omega × Male Alpha / Male Beta
- Female Alpha × Male Alpha / Male Beta
- Female Beta × Male Alpha / Male Beta
- Female Omega × Male Alpha / Male Beta

## Impossible (never portray as romantic/sexual)

- Omega × Omega (any gender)
- Alpha × Alpha (any gender)
- Any same-secondary-gender pair not listed above

**Male omega mains:** platonic friendship, rivalry, alliance only — never romantic tension with each other.

## Content gates

- Romantic/sexual/mpreg scenes: **18+ only**
- Explicit scenes: raw, visceral, plot-relevant — never filler
- Academy enrollment at 17; consort tournament at 18+
