# Omegaverse Rules

Reveal these through protocol, medicine, and behavior. Do not lecture in chapter one.

## Secondary gender

- **Alpha, omega, and beta** are biological categories that present in late adolescence (typically 16–18). They are not personalities.
- **Alphas** run hotter, recover faster, and produce a dominant scent that can sharpen under threat or rut. A trained alpha can hold that scent down. Using it to intimidate is a professional offense in the Meridian Compact’s forces.
- **Omegas** have heats, a more permeable scent profile, and — in this world — no inherent fragility. Ivo is an omega who outwalks half the escort when the science is good.
- **Betas** have no heats or ruts. Their scent is steady and hard to counterfeit. Nia, Rhee, Ellis, Jonah, and Leona are betas. Beta noses are often the best lie detectors on a team because they are not pulled by a heat-scent.

Any adult pairing is legal. Alpha/omega bonds are culturally traditional because heat and rut can lock into a compatible cycle. Alpha/beta, beta/omega, and same-designation marriages are ordinary in cities and still gossiped about in old officer messes. Sable (alpha) and Ellis (beta) are the proof that this story’s love is not a biological assignment.

## Scent, blockers, suppressants

- Field protocol: all alphas and omegas wear **scent blockers** (patch behind the ear, reapplied every twelve hours). Failure to reapply is a safety write-up, not a flirt.
- Omegas on expedition take **military suppressants**. Dr. Jonah Abebe and Sergeant Mateo Solano co-sign the log. A breakthrough heat is a medical evacuation from the line, not a romantic opportunity.
- If suppressants fail, the protection officer who is an alpha **leaves**. Betas hold watch. Calder does this in chapter 21 and does not argue his way back into the tent.
- **Bonding bites** are a documented medical act. Two witnesses, written consent, not during a heat or rut. A protection officer bonding their asset during a detail is a career-ending crime. Calder knows this and treats it as a bright line, not a technicality.

## Military handling

Aegis Detachment Seven is mixed-designation on purpose. Pair-bonds already on the team (Mateo/Anouk, Harun/Jun) are registered. Registered bondmates may deploy together if neither is in the other’s direct medical chain — Mateo may treat Anouk in the field but Jonah reviews any designation-related care. Jun and Harun do not share a fighting hole when one is injured badly enough to throw the other’s judgment; Nia enforces this in chapter 24.

The asset’s secondary gender is a **security fact** (scent trail, heat timing, blackmail risk), not a reason to speak to him as if he is a package that feels.

## What the ruins are not

The Selenqar learned to *speak* scent as a language the Marrow could hear. They did not invent alphas and omegas. Secondary gender exists worldwide, outside the Orrocanopy. Hester floats the wrong theory; Ivo dismantles it.
