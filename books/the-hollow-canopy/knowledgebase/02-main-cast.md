# Main Cast

Ages are at the start of the expedition. School was fourteen years ago at **St. Briony Collegiate**, Harrowgate.

## Protagonists

### Major Calder Rhys — alpha, 32

Commander, Aegis Detachment Seven, Directorate of Anomalous Security. Callsign **Warden**. Decorated. Cocky because the work has backed the jokes up. Loyal to a fault, perceptive about his soldiers, slow about himself.

**School:** Popular, athletic, proud. He and two friends soaked Ivo’s science-fair notebook — a year of moth-wing studies and a clumsy attempt at an untranslated inscription — after Ivo corrected Calder’s answer in front of a history class and people laughed. He shoved Ivo once in the east stair and told him to stay where ghosts belong. He also, once, told a worse boy to walk away, then mocked Ivo the next day so his friends would not smell the kindness. They were paired for three library afternoons on a project about vanished cities. Calder was almost decent. Then he wasn’t.

He does not remember this as a story he stars in. He remembers pencil shavings and a boy who never cried, and he has filed it under “I was a little shit, I grew up.” He has not imagined the size of the wound.

**Now:** Six-foot-three, easy in his body, a pale scar along the jaw from a convoy. Scent, under the blocker: clean steel and bitter citrus. Humor vanishes the second someone is actually about to die. He will not use scent, rank, or rut to move Ivo. When he falls, he falls by memorizing tea, exhaustion, and the lie “I’m fine.”

**Arc:** bully → competent commander → recognition and real guilt → love he does not spend like a weapon → rejection survived without punishment → the wall → the search → vulnerability → partnership he has earned the right to ask for, not the right to keep.

Childhood name only Ivo uses against him later: **Cal**. Calder hated it. His mother used it. The yearbook used it.

### Dr. Ivo Maren — omega, 31

Lead field scientist, Directorate of Anomalous Ecology. Asset designation **ORCHID**. Publishes as **I. Maren**. Anomalous biology, supernatural ecosystems, ancient languages, creature behavior.

**School:** Scholarship student. Quiet, gifted, socially reserved. He noticed Calder with the unhappy accuracy of someone who had to. He did not love the cruelty. He loved, without a word for it, the boy who knew the answer in the library and threw it away in the hall. He has spent fourteen years proving he does not need that boy. He was wrong about the proving. He was not wrong about the hurt.

**Now:** Lean, a shade under six feet, dark hair cut because the jungle will not be romantic about it, a thin scar through the left eyebrow from a centrifuge, not from school. Scent locked down to rain and antiseptic — an absence Calder’s hindbrain keeps hunting. Voice low, pauses before the important sentence, head tilt when he is measuring you. He rarely shouts. When he is afraid he gets quieter. Curiosity beats fear until someone he loves is bleeding; then the scientist leaves the room and the man arrives, badly.

**Arc:** controlled survivor → professional contempt → precise, non-cruel revenge after recognition → trust he resents → rejection from fear (that Calder loves the composure, that accepting him means forgiving too fast, that need is a door he bricked shut) → the empty space where the jokes were → panic → confession → choosing Calder with the past still in the record.

He drinks oversteeped black tea with a crushed green cardamom pod if anyone has one. He lies about sleep. He names specimens before he names his own feelings.

## Aegis Detachment Seven

### Captain Nia Okonkwo — beta, 34 — second-in-command

Practical, sarcastic, the adult in a room Calder is busy being brilliant in. Former urban breach. She lost a civilian asset three years ago by trusting the asset’s confidence over her own perimeter. She overcorrects toward control. She likes Ivo’s competence and hates his wandering. She is the first to see Calder’s tell (he gets funnier when he is in trouble emotionally) and the last to let him confuse guilt with command.

**Arc:** learns that holding a person is not the same as keeping them in the box that would have saved the last one.

### Sergeant Mateo Solano — alpha, 36 — medic

Blunt, warm, incapable of a bedside manner that isn’t also an accusation. Bonded six years to Anouk. The bond is registered, quarrelsome, and kind. He is not allowed to put Anouk above the asset; the one time both are in danger (ch 31–32) he stays with the wounded half of the team because Anouk told him, in advance, that she would not forgive him for choosing her over a kid with a torn artery. There is no kid. There is Harun. He chooses Harun. Anouk survives because she is excellent, not because he hovered.

**Arc:** trust as a practice, not a feeling.

### Corporal Jun Park — omega, 29 — sniper

Few words, vicious accuracy, a private sense of humor that only Harun hears at full volume. Bonded to Harun. Shoulder wound in chapter 24 (Helix round, through-and-through, no bone). Still shoots left-handed-braced after. Hates being benched and accepts it when Nia makes Harun stand down with him, because she will not let a bond throw two guns away.

**Arc:** lets himself be protected without calling it weakness.

### Sergeant Harun Dalca — alpha, 33 — heavy weapons

Loud, loyal, jokes too large for the room. Bonded to Jun. Claustrophobic after a cousin died in a mine collapse; he has told Jun and no one else until the cistern stair (ch 16), where he goes down anyway because Jun is already below. Leg pinned by stone in the final collapse (ch 43). He keeps the leg. He does not keep the illusion that courage feels like calm.

**Arc:** fear walked through, not erased.

### Lieutenant Sable Venn — alpha, 30 — reconnaissance

Competitive, clean lines, a diplomat’s daughter who washed out of the desk track on purpose. Distrusts scientists after an analyst’s “low risk” note walked her old team into a nest. Ellis Ward is therefore intolerable: he walks toward animals. She falls in love with the way he is right about them.

**Arc:** Clash → reliance → a love that has no heat-cycle to blame. They are not bonded by the epilogue. They are unmistakable.

### Specialist Rhee Amari — beta, 28 — combat engineer and communications

She/her. Brilliant with radios and structural lies (the good kind: shoring, charges, bridges). Her sister is a journalist. Rhee left a letter that says nothing prosecutable and everything frightened. In chapter 6 she copies a ghost signal; in chapter 14 she admits she sat on a Helix burst for six hours because she wanted to crack it herself. That delay costs them surprise. She spends the rest of the book paying it back in working gear and one honest report.

**Arc:** cleverness submits to the team.

## The science team

### Dr. Anouk Solano (née Beltran) — omega, 35 — archaeologist

Passionate, argumentative, allergic to vague mysticism, married to the evidence and to Mateo. She and Hester fight about whether a mural is ritual or lab notebook. Anouk is usually righter and worse at saying so kindly. She does not know the school story. She knows Ivo from a conference and trusts his nerve more than his self-preservation.

### Dr. Priya Raman — omega, 33 — linguist

Ivo’s friend. Keeper of the secret. Dry, precise, braver about other people’s hearts than her own. She wants the Selenqari knot-script the way some people want air. When she realizes a full translation of the asking-wheel would teach Helix how to ring the Bell, she mistranslates the weaponizable stanza in the official copy and tells Ivo what she did. He backs her. Fear: being the person who handed a god a grammar. She is not in love with Ivo. Calder briefly thinks she is (ch 23) and is wrong.

### Dr. Ellis Ward — beta, 34 — field biologist

Eccentric, kind to specimens, talks to moths as if they might answer. Afraid he is dead weight in a fight. He is the reason the team survives the Mirror Hind and, later, why Sable believes a beta who cannot shoot can still keep her alive. Cardamom in his kit because Ivo once mentioned it and Ellis is the sort of man who remembers other people’s small hungers.

### Dr. Jonah Abebe — beta, 40 — medical researcher

Suppressants, venom, and the ethics of both. Friendly with Mateo, impatient with heroics. After the scent-leeches he rewrites the medical protocol in a night. No romance. He is the one who tells Ivo, during the vigil, to sleep in a chair if he will not leave, and then pretends not to see the hand-holding.

### Professor Hester Lang — alpha, 58 — historian and occultist

Magnificent, stubborn, smells like paper and clove even through a blocker she resents wearing. She knew **Dr. Pavel Ruiz** of Survey Lantern and has carried his “lost to rapids” file as an insult for eleven years. She wants the site preserved. The military wants it contained. She is right that burning knowledge is a kind of death and wrong that the Bell is a library instead of a mouth.

### Captain Tomas Ibarra — alpha, 38 — Virelian liaison escort

Local military, respectful, interested in Ivo in an ordinary adult way. Ivo is polite and unmoved. Calder is unpleasant about it and then ashamed. Tomas takes his people back to the river when the Reach starts answering questions (after ch 16). Not a villain. A door Calder hates seeing open.

## Antagonists

### Director Adrien Cho — alpha, 51 — Helix Meridian

Former Compact cartographer. He believes the Marrow is a tool that could end drought and disease, and that leaving it in a jungle is ethics as vanity. He will spend the expedition to ring the Bell. He is half-completed when he does, and lives, horribly, long enough to understand he was answered.

### Captain Leona Varga — beta, 36 — Helix field commander

Her younger brother died in an anomaly the Compact classified and walked away from. She wants the knowledge so no other family gets a redacted sentence instead of a reason. She will not shoot a wounded sniper who is already down (ch 24). She helps seal the Bell. She still steals the ecology — Ivo lets the theft be smaller than she planned. Sympathetic, not absolved.

## The dead

### Dr. Pavel Ruiz

Hester’s colleague. Survey Lantern. He understood, too late, that the Marrow finishes patterns. His last useful act was the warning the radio still repeats.
