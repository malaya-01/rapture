# Volume 3 — What He Carries

Chapters 19–27. Act III. End on the wall.

## Chapter 19 — After the Name
**POV:** Calder. Guilt without romance to wash it. He tries to apologize in the mouth of the gallery. Ivo refuses the scene: “You don’t get to be forgiven where it’s cinematic.” Calder accepts the hit. He does not explain the bullying as misunderstood affection. He says he was cruel and proud and that he had not thought about the size of it. Ivo says, “I know. That’s the part I can’t stand.”

## Chapter 20 — Technically Correct
**POV:** Ivo. Revenge, small and exact. He calls him Cal when they are alone. He answers questions literally. He makes Calder carry the stylus case. He pretends not to understand a joke. He reminds him of the yearbook photo. It is not kind. It is not abuse. Calder, to his own disgust, likes the attention. Nia tells him to get his face under control.

## Chapter 21 — Leave the Tent
**POV:** Jonah. Scent-thieves. False heat-signals in two omegas, Ivo included. Suppressants hold the biology; they do not hold the panic. Protocol: alphas out. Calder goes without bargaining, posts Nia and Jonah, stands in the rain until his own hands stop wanting to be useful. Ivo, inside, hates the correctness and trusts it later, which is worse.

## Chapter 22 — The Laugh
**POV:** Calder. A miserable morning. Ivo, documenting a lumen-moth with Priya, laughs — startled, real, not a weapon. Calder feels the laugh land in his ribs and knows the word for it. He does not say it. He starts lying to his team so badly that Harun and Jun take bets.

## Chapter 23 — Too Close
**POV:** Ivo. He notices the jacket-pattern, the tea, the way Calder stands between him and Tomas Ibarra without making it an order. Jealousy misfiled: he catches Calder watching Priya and him and is angry at being misunderstood, then angry at caring. Tomas is decent. Ivo sends him nothing. Calder is unbearable and then, at night, ashamed in a conversation with Nia that Ivo overhears one sentence of: “He isn’t a debt.”

## Chapter 24 — Down
**POV:** Jun. Helix contact. Leona’s people. A real firefight in the trees. Jun takes the shoulder round. Leona has a clear second shot on a downed omega and does not take it — she wants the Bell, not a massacre she can’t justify to the brother she still talks to. Mateo works. Sable is clipped dizzy. Rhee’s counter-net saves the evacuation route. The delay from chapter 14 is why Helix was on the ridge.

## Chapter 25 — Not While I Wear This
**POV:** Calder. Night watch with Ivo. Honesty about the library afternoons, incomplete, no excuse. They are close enough to kiss. Calder stops. He names the power imbalance and the detail and the fact that Ivo has not asked. Ivo is shaken that the stop is real. This is the chapter where Ivo starts to be afraid he wants him.

## Chapter 26 — I’m Not Asking You to Say It Back
**POV:** Ivo. Calder confesses: love, specific, no demand, no scent, no bargain. Ivo rejects him. Reasons he says out loud: he will not be the proof that Calder turned out good; he is afraid the love is for the calm man and not for the boy who was hurt; he cannot forgive on a schedule; if he needs Calder he will not survive being wrong. “I can’t reciprocate. Not now. Don’t make me the villain of your restraint.” Calder agrees. He does not argue.

## Chapter 27 — Orders
**POV:** Nia. The next day Calder runs the team like a man who has put his heart in a locked case. Polite to Ivo. No jokes. No extra closeness. No punishment. The science gets cleaner and the camp gets colder. Ivo tells Priya this is what he wanted. Priya says, “Then why are you waiting for him to come over here?” End of Act III.
