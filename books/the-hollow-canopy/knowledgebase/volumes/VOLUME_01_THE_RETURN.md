# Volume 1 — The Return

Chapters 1–9. Act I. End on the causeway stones.

## Chapter 1 — The Man They Sent
**POV:** Calder. **Day 0.** Base Lantern before dawn. Team banter, blockers as routine, the ORCHID packet (Dr. I. Maren, photo that looks past the camera). Rhee logs the ghost signal every nineteen minutes: *don’t ask it.* Calder files it as a bad radio and a worse omen. He expects an arrogant, fragile academic. Rotors on the horizon.

## Chapter 2 — The Initial
**POV:** Ivo. **Day 0.** Helicopter. He has seen the roster. Priya knows. He forbids the name. Memory: the soaked notebook, moth wings bleeding ink, Calder’s laugh — not desire, damage. He lands, sees the man, and introduces himself as Dr. Maren. Calder does not know him. Ivo is almost offended by the relief, and then by the lack of it.

## Chapter 3 — Asset Orchid
**POV:** Calder. **Day 0.** Briefing. Ivo corrects the threat model without raising his voice. Déjà vu: the pause, the head tilt. Calder shakes it off. Priya’s face does something Ivo kills with a look. First professional friction. Calder assigns himself as personal security and calls it babysitting where Ivo can hear. Ivo says, “Then try to keep up.”

## Chapter 4 — People You Mean to Bring Back
**POV:** Nia. **Day 1.** River staging. She reads both teams. Mateo and Anouk’s reunion is a forehead touch and a logistics argument — love that already knows how to work. Ellis and Sable clash over a live specimen case. Hester hates the military tents. Rhee shows Nia the signal log. Nia believes it more than Calder does. Rain becomes the climate, not the weather.

## Chapter 5 — Scientifically Acceptable
**POV:** Ivo. **Day 2.** Upriver. Lumen-moths, stone in the wing veins, trees ringed like music. Ivo crosses a perimeter tape because a moth line goes through it. The argument: ordered to stay / yes / you crossed / yes / why / the answer was on the other side / not acceptable / scientifically acceptable. Calder makes him eat. Ivo hates that his hands shake only after, and that Priya sees. Calder does not. Familiarity without a name: Ivo’s way of waiting a beat before he speaks.

## Chapter 6 — What the Rapids Didn’t Do
**POV:** Sable. **Day 3.** Survey Lantern’s camp, half-swallowed. Journal: Pavel Ruiz writing that the river is repeating them. Official story was rapids. Ellis reads the animal sign and is right. Sable hates that she needs him. A hind-print that is almost a boot. They do not yet know the name Mirror Hind. Hester identifies Pavel’s hand and goes quiet in a way that scares Anouk more than shouting would.

## Chapter 7 — Look at Its Movement
**POV:** Calder. **Day 4.** Dusk. Glass kites. Everyone says run. Ivo says wait, look at the leading edge. Calder drags him anyway — and uses what Ivo saw: they dive along scent, and the rain is starting. Jun’s shot, placed where Ivo pointed, turns a slaughter into a wound. Jun grazed. Ellis’s ankle. Calder’s respect arrives as anger. After, Ivo’s pause before answering feels like a room Calder has stood in.

## Chapter 8 — Not on the Chart
**POV:** Mateo. **Day 4 night.** He treats Jun and Ellis. Jonah reviews suppressant logs because kite-scent shook two alphas harder than it should have. Anouk and Hester fight over a basin carving. Mateo and Anouk, in their tent: how to be married when one of you is the mission’s medic and the other is a person the mission will spend. Calder asks if ORCHID is always like this. Mateo: “He’s scared. He does it quietly. You didn’t hear that from me.”

## Chapter 9 — The First Stones
**POV:** Ivo. **Day 6.** The causeway. Qalen-Sar under the roots. The Vein in his teeth. He looks at Calder’s back and almost says the name, because the city feels like the library project they failed. He doesn’t. They go in. Act I ends.
