# Volume 5 — The Last Question

Chapters 37–45. Act V. The adventure is not over because the feelings are.

## Chapter 37 — Still Us
**POV:** Calder. They are together and still themselves. He is cocky at half volume because of the sling. Ivo is composed and, only with him, obvious. A fight about whether a wounded commander enters the Bell court. Ivo wins on medicine. Calder wins on the map. They both go, which is the compromise of people who have stopped pretending one of them is in charge of the other’s soul.

## Chapter 38 — Her Brother
**POV:** Leona. Her philosophy and her limit. She parleys. Cho will ring the Bell at dawn regardless of the Unfinished, regardless of the coast. She will help stop the ring if Ivo promises the ecological record survives. Ivo promises a record that cannot teach the ask. Leona accepts, furious and grateful. Calder does not trust her. He trusts Ivo’s terms.

## Chapter 39 — The Listening Court
**POV:** Priya. They walk the murals. Triad, Vein, the question about death. She translates aloud. The Unfinished stand in the water to listen to their own names, the ones she can give back. Some remain unfinished. Mercy is partial. She decides, here, to break the weapon stanza in the official copy.

## Chapter 40 — Lantern
**POV:** Hester. Pavel’s full loop, still on the air because the Marrow completes transmissions. The rapids story dies. Hester weeps once, angrily. The ghost signal from chapter 1 is this. She votes to seal the Bell and hates herself for agreeing with a major.

## Chapter 41 — The Mouth
**POV:** Ivo. The Bell chamber. The scientific explanation, discovered, not lectured: vats, wheel, cistern, the city’s bones in the roots. He could ask a small question. A cure. He feels the shape of it in his teeth and sets his hand down without ringing. Calder sees the choice and does not grab his wrist. That is why Ivo can choose.

## Chapter 42 — Cho
**POV:** Calder. The fight. Tactics with one good arm, Nia running the line, Sable and Ellis cutting the eel-gate the wrong way on purpose so metal-song pulls the guardians between Helix and the wheel. Cho reaches the striker.

## Chapter 43 — Half
**POV:** Ivo. Cho strikes a cracked bell. The Marrow begins to finish him — not a cinematic death, a horrible partial answer. Ivo and Priya flood the resonance. Rhee blows the wheel. Harun’s leg is pinned shielding Jun and he lives. They drag Cho out because leaving him as a sample would make them the thing he is. Leona covers the retreat and is gone by the river with the notes Ivo let her see, not the stanza.

## Chapter 44 — The Record
**POV:** Ivo. Extraction. He and Calder, bandaged, honest. Calder’s guilt is not a speech Ivo has to absolve. Ivo: he does not forget the notebook, the stair, the laugh in the hall. He is choosing the man who carried the jacket and left the tent and heard the worst of him without using it. They are not fixed. They are partnered. Puerto Virel’s lights are an offense and a mercy.

## Chapter 45 — One Note Higher
**POV:** Calder. Six months later. Classified hearings. Jun’s shoulder. Harun’s limp and his joke about it. Mateo and Anouk on leave, still arguing about murals. Sable and Ellis sharing a flat and a moth terrarium. Priya’s career in a quiet room with a locked drawer. Leona unfound. Cho in a medical vault, still trying to finish a sentence with a mouth that isn’t only his. Ivo in Calder’s kitchen, stealing the good tea, insulting his report’s prose. The listening post calls. The Vein, offshore, has changed pitch. Somewhere, a new question. Calder looks at Ivo. Ivo says, “Not tonight.” The novel ends on the kettle, and the hum under it.
