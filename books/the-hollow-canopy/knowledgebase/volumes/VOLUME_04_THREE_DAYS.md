# Volume 4 — Three Days

Chapters 28–36. Act IV. Emotional climax.

## Chapter 28 — He Doesn’t Ask
**POV:** Ivo. Calder no longer asks if he has eaten, no longer complains about the lab wander, no longer smiles at insults. Ivo is safe and miserable. He does the work. A new mural shows Wardens who were gardeners. He almost tells Calder and swallows it because he asked for this silence.

## Chapter 29 — The Unfinished
**POV:** Hester. They meet the partial people. A voice from the wrong angle knows Hester’s name, then Ivo’s, then Calder’s, because the Vein repeats introductions. The Unfinished want the question completed so they can stop. Hester wants to grant them personhood by translation. Ivo wants more data before mercy becomes a mistake. Calder, professional, sets a watch and does not look at Ivo longer than tactics require. It guts Ivo more than the ghosts.

## Chapter 30 — The Empty Side
**POV:** Ivo. He admits to himself, not yet to Calder, that he misses being annoyed with love in it. He eats without being told and resents the food. Ellis and Sable share a watch and a first, careful kiss that Ivo witnesses and does not mock. The contrast is unbearable. He almost crosses the camp to speak. Helix’s flare goes up instead.

## Chapter 31 — The Break
**POV:** Nia. Assault plus a Vein-storm. The basin collapses. Teams split: Nia with Rhee, Harun, a wounded path; Mateo pulled to Harun’s side later; Anouk with Priya, Hester, Jonah in a side stair; Sable and Ellis on the ridge; Calder and Ivo together for nine minutes and then not. Radios die into Pavel’s cadence. Nia does her job. She does not know yet that Ivo is gone from Calder’s reach.

## Chapter 32 — Say It
**POV:** Calder. He is calm while the soldiers are missing in known patterns. Then Ivo is not on any pattern. Something in him breaks without becoming stupid: he searches flooded tunnels, a kite nest, burning canopy, the nun-court. His people try to stop him. He refuses and still gives Nia a rally point so the refusal isn’t suicide for the team. He finds Ivo under a falling comb of stone, a glass kite in the smoke. He gets Ivo out. The kite opens his ribs. A block shears his forearm. He stays conscious long enough to say, “You’re all right. Don’t do the quiet thing.”

## Chapter 33 — Don’t You Dare
**POV:** Ivo. The scientist ends. He says Calder’s name like a person. His hands shake. He cries. He will not leave for the rally point until Mateo — arriving from the other hell — physically promises a litter. The mission is noise. Only the frightened man is left, and he has loved this one person, badly and for years.

## Chapter 34 — Day One
**POV:** Jonah. Field hospital in a Keeper hall. Calder unconscious, stable if the word is kind. Ivo refuses the cot. He reports the mission to a man who cannot hear it: moths, glyphs, Helix, the laugh he didn’t mean to give. The team understands. Harun, in pain, tells Jun, “That’s not an asset.” Jun says, “It hasn’t been.”

## Chapter 35 — Day Three
**POV:** Ivo. He breaks. Hand, chest, tears. He confesses the years: loved before he had the word, through the cruelty, after the distance, the rejection as fear, the plea to wake up. He does not know Calder has been awake since the sentence about the moths.

## Chapter 36 — Won
**POV:** Calder. He waited because interrupting the truth would have been another theft. At the peak, weak, smiling: “So. Does this mean I finally won?” Ivo’s mortification. The almost-murder. The hug that hurts the ribs and is not released. “Don’t ever do that again.” “I won’t.” He means dying. He also means leaving the room where Ivo is telling the truth. End of Act IV.
