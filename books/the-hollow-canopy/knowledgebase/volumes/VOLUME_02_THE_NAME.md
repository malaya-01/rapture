# Volume 2 — The Name

Chapters 10–18. Act II. End on recognition.

## Chapter 10 — The Same Knot
**POV:** Priya. **Day 7.** First readable glyph. Ask and finish are one word. She doesn’t say the doom yet. She says the dictionary is wrong or the city was. Anouk and Hester take sides. Priya watches Calder watch Ivo and understands the shape of a disaster that isn’t linguistic.

## Chapter 11 — Lullaby
**POV:** Ivo. **Day 8.** Root nuns. The song is a Keeper lullaby with the grammar broken. He wants to record it. Calder wants salt and noise and distance. They do both, in that order, because Jun is already kneeling and smiling at nothing. Ivo’s science saves Jun’s head. Calder’s tactics save Ivo’s. Neither thanks the other cleanly.

## Chapter 12 — Pencil Shavings
**POV:** Calder. **Day 9.** A rain-slip of his blocker. Citrus and steel for one breath. Ivo goes still. Calder doesn’t know why. Déjà vu becomes specific: a classroom window, someone not crying. He cannot hold it. He re-patches and is short with everyone. Nia notices.

## Chapter 13 — Ask It Something
**POV:** Ellis. **Day 10.** A Mirror Hind wears Sable’s posture and repeats her last order as if it were a person. Ellis, remembering Ivo’s lecture nobody wanted, asks the thing a question. It cannot answer. The mimic drops. Sable looks at Ellis as if the jungle has changed genus. This is the turn in their romance: respect first.

## Chapter 14 — Six Hours
**POV:** Rhee. **Day 11.** She confesses the Helix burst she sat on. Nia’s anger is quiet, which is worse. Calder takes the blame upward and gives Rhee the fix downward: she will build the counter-net, and she will not be clever alone again. A Helix drone dies in the canopy. Leona’s scouts now know the expedition is ahead of them and wounded in its trust.

## Chapter 15 — Cardamom
**POV:** Calder. **Day 12.** Ivo sleeps on the notes. Calder puts his jacket over him. Ivo does not wake. Ellis, on watch, sees and says nothing except, later, to offer cardamom as if it were not a conspiracy. Calder learns the tea. He tells himself it is asset management. He is a bad liar and only Nia is qualified to grade him.

## Chapter 16 — Cache the Metal
**POV:** Harun. **Day 13.** The Cistern Stair. His fear. Jun already below. Eels rise at rifles and radios. Ivo understands they are guarding silence. Calder orders the cache. Harun goes down without his gun and hates every step and does it. They find vats grown for questions, not for fish. A bone stylus that records living sound. On it, a fragment of Pavel’s voice: *don’t ask it what it wants.*

## Chapter 17 — The Gallery
**POV:** Ivo. **Day 14.** A mural sequence is flaking. He crosses a cracked span because the last panel is the verb he’s been chasing. The stone goes. Calder hauls him and they fight while the gallery sheds itself. Ivo, furious and for the first time uncomposed: “You’ve always been like this.”

## Chapter 18 — Ivo
**POV:** Calder. **Day 14.** The sentence hits the classroom, the uniform, the notebook, the stair. He says, “Ivo.” Ivo goes still. Recognition is mutual. Rain through the broken roof. Neither of them has a protocol for this. End of Act II.
