# Premise and Pacing

One novel. Five volumes, one per act. Nine chapters each. Target about 2,500–3,200 words a chapter when the scene can bear it. Do not pad. Do not repeat the same argument.

POV rotates with purpose. Calder and Ivo carry the romance. Side POVs carry couple-arcs, tactics, and clues the leads cannot see.

## Act I — The Return (ch 1–9)

Introduce Calder, Ivo, both teams, the Compact’s professionalism, the assignment, the first meeting, Ivo’s silence, Calder’s failure to know him, and the jungle as a body. End on the first stones of Qalen-Sar.

## Act II — The Name (ch 10–18)

Ruins, creatures, the ask/finish glyph, side romances in motion, Calder’s déjà vu, Ivo’s cold accuracy (resentment before it becomes deliberate revenge). End with the gallery collapse and Calder saying “Ivo.”

## Act III — What He Carries (ch 19–27)

Guilt, Ivo’s precise payback, deepening horror, Helix, the leech/false-heat test of consent, Calder falling in love, confession, rejection, the wall.

## Act IV — Three Days (ch 28–36)

Distance that hurts, the Unfinished, the assault, separation, the search, the injury, Ivo’s break, the vigil, the confession, the joke that proves Calder heard it.

## Act V — The Last Question (ch 37–45)

They go back as themselves, only honest. The city explains itself. Cho rings what he should not. They seal it. They leave. Six months later the hum changes.

## Relationship rules by phase

- **Before ch 18:** Ivo is cold and flawless. He does not hint for the reader in dialogue Calder can decode. POV-Ivo may remember. POV-Calder may only feel familiarity.
- **Ch 19–25:** Ivo’s revenge is irritating, accurate, and not abusive. Calder enjoys it and is ashamed of enjoying it. No kiss.
- **Ch 26:** Calder confesses with no demand. Ivo refuses with a real reason. Calder accepts.
- **Ch 27–31:** Calder is an excellent officer and a closed door. He does not punish.
- **Ch 36:** First mutual embrace. Still not a different couple. The banter returns with blood on it.
- **After:** Arguments continue. Softness is specific to each other, not a personality transplant.

## Injury and gear locks

- Blockers: twelve-hour patches. Rain degrades the edges. Calder’s slips in ch 12 for a minute; Ivo goes very still and says nothing.
- Ivo’s tea tin. Ellis’s cardamom. Calder learns both.
- Jun’s rifle is *Ash*. Harun’s gun is *Door*. Rhee’s primary radio is *Lantern-2*; the ghost rides the old survey band, not their encrypted net, until Helix bridges them.
- Metal cache before the eels (ch 16). Anyone who cheats this gets hurt.
- Calder after ch 32 cannot fight one-armed without cost. Act V tactics must respect the sling and the ribs.
