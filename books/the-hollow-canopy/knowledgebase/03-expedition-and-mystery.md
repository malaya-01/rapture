# Expedition, Civilization, Creatures, Mystery

## The ground

- **Virelia** — equatorial republic. The interior is unmapped by treaty as much as by terrain.
- **Puerto Virel** — last real port.
- **Forward Base Lantern** — coastal staging camp, named with bad optimism after the lost survey.
- **The Orrocanopy** — the rainforest. Rain is a personality. Rivers run the wrong temperature. Radios die in pockets and then speak in a dead man’s cadence.
- **The Black Orchid Reach** — the interior where the Vein is loud.
- **Qalen-Sar** — the Drowned Court. Stone platforms, roof combs, causeways, and ritual basins, strangled by trees and half-flooded. Inspired by Mesoamerican mass and geometry, not a copy of any real people.
- **The Cistern Stair** — flooded laboratories.
- **The Listening Court** — mural chamber where the triad is explained by walking it.
- **The Marrow Bell** — the asking-chamber under the court.

## The Selenqar Concord

People: the Selenqar. Language: **Selenqari**, written as **knot-glyphs** — stone cords read by touch and by the angle of shadow. Priya is one of three living readers, and the other two are not on this expedition.

**Myth they told themselves:** the **Listening Rain** is the membrane between lives. The low hum in bone and water is **the Vein**.

**Government:** a triad, not a crown.
- **Speaker** — voice outward
- **Keeper** — memory
- **Warden** — the body of the city, a gardener of dangerous biology more than a soldier

Calder’s callsign is an accident that Ivo does not find funny, and then does.

**What they knew:** scent as syntax; cisterns that stored biological signal; bred guardians; the Marrow under the water table, which completes patterns. They asked it to end death. It completed *them*.

The word for **ask** and the word for **finish** are the same knot. That is the clue that reorganizes every earlier symbol.

Not every encounter is a boss fight. Moths are wonder. Stags are weather with antlers. The Unfinished are a moral problem.

## Clue chain

1. Ghost signal at Base Lantern, every nineteen minutes: *don’t ask it.* (ch 1, source explained ch 40)
2. Trees with rings that are intervals, not years. Moths with stone in the wing. (ch 5)
3. Survey Lantern camp: journal contradicts “lost to rapids.” (ch 6)
4. Knot-glyph: ask = finish. (ch 10)
5. Root-nun song is grammar, not noise. (ch 11)
6. A hind repeats a soldier’s sentence and fails Ivo’s question. (ch 13)
7. Helix is already inside the radio weather. (ch 14)
8. Flooded lab: they grew questions in vats. Eels hate metal because metal is a crude shout. (ch 16)
9. The Unfinished know the expedition’s names because the Vein repeats patterns, including introductions. (ch 29)
10. Pavel’s full warning, still broadcasting because the Marrow is completing the transmission. (ch 40)
11. Murals: the triad asked one question. The city answered by becoming it. (ch 39–41)

## Helix Meridian

Private military-science firm, former Compact contractor, cut loose after an ethics breach. They want the Bell for controllable biology — soldiers who do not die, crops that do not fail, and a lever over anything that lives. Cho frames this as responsibility. Leona frames it as grief. Both will kill for it. Only one of them can be argued with.
