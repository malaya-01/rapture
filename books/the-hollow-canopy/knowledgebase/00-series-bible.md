# The Hollow Canopy — Series Bible

**Spoilers for the entire novel.**

## Logline

Fourteen years after he helped make a quiet boy’s school years smaller, Major Calder Rhys is sent to keep a classified omega scientist alive in an unmapped rainforest. Dr. Ivo Maren knows him at once and says nothing. Under the canopy, a dead civilization is still trying to finish the question that ended it — and the man who used to be cruel is the one person Ivo cannot reduce to a specimen.

## The answer (do not reveal early)

The **Selenqar Concord** was not destroyed by war. Their science spoke to **the Marrow**, a pattern-completing intelligence in the water table of the Orrocanopy. It is not a god. It finishes whatever pattern it is given, in living flesh.

They asked how to end death. The Marrow answered by *completing* them: folding the city of **Qalen-Sar** into a single unfinished organism. The rainforest is the sentence they did not get to close. The creatures are organs that forgot they belonged to a body.

The **Marrow Bell** is the cistern-heart that can ask a new question. Ringing it for immortality, weapons, or a cure would give the Marrow a pattern large enough to finish the coast, then the continent’s living systems.

**The choice:** Calder and Ivo seal the Bell (flood the resonance, break the asking-wheel, bury the question). They keep ecological notes that describe the Reach without teaching anyone how to ask. Director Adrien Cho tries to ring it and is half-completed — evacuated as a warning, not a trophy. Captain Leona Varga helps seal it, then disappears with the non-weaponized notes Ivo chooses to let her carry. Whether that was mercy or a mistake is left uncomfortable.

A previous expedition, **Survey Lantern**, asked a small frightened question (“what is killing us?”). The Marrow is still repeating their last radio words. That repetition is the early ghost signal: *don’t ask it.*

## Ending

They walk out. The childhood record is not erased. Ivo’s line, in substance: he does not forget, and he is still choosing Calder. Epilogue, six months later: the team is alive and altered; the file is classified; a coastal listening post hears the Vein hum one note higher. Somewhere else, someone has asked. This novel is still complete.

## What Calder does not know at the start

He does not know asset ORCHID is Ivo Maren. The dossier says **Dr. I. Maren**. At St. Briony he mocked a boy he thought of as the scholarship ghost; the surname never stuck. He will not connect the face. Ivo introduces himself as Dr. Maren and withholds “Ivo” until the recognition scene forces it.

## Who knows the school history

- Ivo: from the start.
- Priya Raman: told once, years ago, when Ivo was drunk and precise. She recognizes Calder at the briefing and keeps silent because Ivo looks at her once.
- Everyone else: ignorant until chapter 18, then only what they overhear or are told. Nia learns enough to be angry on Ivo’s behalf and still follow Calder. She does not forgive Calder *for* Ivo.

## Thematic law

People can change and still owe the past. Protection is not permission. Love after resentment is a decision both of them make while competent, frightened, and unreconciled.
