# Content Maturity (18+)

The Hollow Canopy is **adult fiction**.

## Include honestly

- Military violence, injury, and creature horror when the expedition demands it
- Adult romantic and sexual tension between Calder (32) and Ivo (31) only after trust exists; the relationship is not consummated before the emotional climax and the start of their actual romance
- Heat, rut, suppressants, and scent as medical and social facts, never as consent
- Childhood bullying in memory: humiliation, exclusion, a ruined project, a shove. It was harmful. It was not flirtation.

## Do not

- Sexualize minors or write the school years as romance
- Use “I bullied you because I wanted you”
- Use pheromones, heat, or command presence to override a no
- Make Ivo secretly submissive or Calder’s love a redemption prize he is owed
- Linger on torture

## Tone

Oppressive beauty, dry humor, fear that goes quiet instead of loud. The ending is hopeful and unfinished only in the way a real world is unfinished: this expedition is complete.
