# Master Prompt — The Hollow Canopy

Operational law for prose lives in `knowledgebase/INSTRUCTIONS.md` and the bible. The novel this prompt demands is a complete adult expedition romance, not a synopsis.

## Must hold

- Adults only in every romantic beat. Calder Rhys is 32. Ivo Maren is 31.
- School bullying was real harm. Never romanticize it. Never “I was cruel because I liked you.”
- Slow burn. Calder falls first. Ivo rejects him for a true reason. Calder accepts and goes distant without punishment. Ivo confesses during the three-day vigil. Calder heard him. The line is: “So. Does this mean I finally won?”
- No pheromone coercion. No heat as consent. Calder leaves the tent in the false-heat chapter.
- The Marrow finishes patterns in living flesh. The Selenqar asked how to end death and were completed. The Bell must be sealed. The cure is refused.
- Ask and finish are the same knot-glyph.
- The ghost signal *don’t ask it* is Survey Lantern, still repeating because the Marrow completes transmissions.
- Side romances: Mateo Solano and Anouk Solano (established bond); Harun Dalca and Jun Park (established bond); Sable Venn and Ellis Ward (slow, alpha/beta). Priya Raman knows the school history and is not a love interest.
- One POV per chapter. Write scenes. Do not summarize the chapter you are in.
- Track the sling and the ribs after chapter 32. Track Jun’s shoulder after chapter 24. Track Harun’s leg after chapter 43.

## Do not contradict

`00-series-bible.md`, `02-main-cast.md`, `03-expedition-and-mystery.md`, and the volume beats.
